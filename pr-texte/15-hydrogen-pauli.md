# feat(PhyslibAlpha): Pauli's algebraic derivation of the hydrogen spectrum

Branch: `hydrogen-pauli` · Base: master · Depends on: nothing open

This PR formalises Pauli's 1926 derivation of the hydrogen bound-state energies from the commutation relations alone. A `PauliSystem` consists of operators `Lᵢ`, `Aᵢ`, `H` on a complex vector space satisfying the relations of `LaplaceRungeLenzVector.lean` at `ε → 0` in dimension 3, together with `L · A = 0`. On an eigenspace of `H` with eigenvalue `E < 0`, the operators `Jᵢ = (Lᵢ + Aᵢ/√(-2mE)) / (2ℏ)` form an angular momentum representation whose Casimir is the scalar `(-ℏ² - m k²/(2E)) / (4ℏ²)`. Comparing with the value `j(j+1)` from the `sl₂` classification gives `E = -m k² / (2ℏ² n²)`, i.e. a Bohr level `HydrogenAtom.energyLevel`.

## Main results

- `PauliSystem.exists_energyLevel`: every nonzero finite-dimensional eigenspace of `H` with eigenvalue `E < 0` has `E = Hy.energyLevel n` for some `n` (listed in `Theorems.txt`).
- `PauliSystem.comm_J`: `[Jᵢ, Jᵢ₊₁] = i Jᵢ₊₂` on a space where `H = E < 0`.
- `PauliSystem.plusRep_Jsq`: `J² = (-ℏ² - m k²/(2E)) / (4ℏ²)` there.

## Notes

- The argument is abstract. That physlib's regularised operators `𝐋`, `𝐀(ε)`, `𝐇(ε)` give a `PauliSystem` in the limit `ε → 0` (on a suitable domain) is not proved; in particular `L · A = 0` is an assumption.
- Only `d = 3`. Only the energies are derived, not the degeneracy `n²` of the levels.
- `exists_Jsq_eigenvector` is stated in the namespace `PhyslibAlpha.Sl2.AngularMomentumRepresentation`.
